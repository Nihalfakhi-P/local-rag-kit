# Northwind Robotics — Leave Policy

*Fictional sample document for local-rag-kit demos.*

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues monthly at 2 days per month. Part-time employees receive leave pro-rated to their contracted hours.

Up to 5 unused annual leave days can be carried over into the next calendar year. Carried-over days expire on March 31 if they are not used.

Annual leave requests must be submitted in the HR portal at least 10 working days in advance for absences longer than three days. Shorter absences need 2 working days' notice.

## Sick leave

Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for sick leave longer than 2 consecutive days. Sick leave does not carry over.

## Parental leave

Primary caregivers receive 26 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 6 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Parental leave can start up to 4 weeks before the expected due date.

## Public holidays

Northwind Robotics observes 10 public holidays per year. The list is published in the HR portal every December for the following year.
