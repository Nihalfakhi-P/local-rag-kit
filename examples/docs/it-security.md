# Northwind Robotics — IT and Security Handbook

*Fictional sample document for local-rag-kit demos.*

## Passwords and MFA

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for email, the HR portal, Ledgerly, and the source-code platform. Hardware security keys are issued to all engineers and anyone with production access.

## Lost or stolen devices

Report a lost or stolen laptop or phone to the security team within 1 hour by emailing security@northwind.example or calling the 24/7 security line. IT remotely wipes the device after it is reported.

## VPN error codes

- **E-4021**: your device certificate has expired. Open the Northwind Device app and click "Renew certificate", then reconnect.
- **E-4033**: your account is locked after 5 failed sign-in attempts. It unlocks automatically after 30 minutes, or the IT help desk can unlock it sooner.
- **E-5100**: the VPN gateway is under maintenance. Maintenance windows are Sundays from 02:00 to 04:00 UTC.

## Software installation

Only software from the Northwind Self-Service catalogue may be installed on company laptops. Requests for software not in the catalogue go through the IT help desk and are reviewed by security within 5 working days.

## Data classification

Data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential, or Restricted. Restricted data, such as customer robot telemetry, must never be copied to personal devices or uploaded to external AI tools.
