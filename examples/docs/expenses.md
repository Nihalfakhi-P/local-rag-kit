# Northwind Robotics — Travel and Expense Policy

*Fictional sample document for local-rag-kit demos.*

## Submitting expenses

Expense claims must be submitted in the Ledgerly app within 30 days of the purchase. Every claim over 25 US dollars needs an itemised receipt. Claims submitted after 60 days are rejected automatically.

Approved claims are reimbursed in the next payroll cycle. Payroll runs on the last working day of each month.

## Travel

Economy class is required for flights shorter than 6 hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights of 6 hours or longer. Business class requires written approval from a vice president.

The hotel limit is 180 US dollars per night in standard cities and 260 US dollars per night in high-cost cities such as New York, London, Zurich, and Singapore.

## Meals

The daily meal allowance while travelling is 75 US dollars. Alcohol is not reimbursable. Client entertainment must be pre-approved by your manager and logged with the names of all attendees.

## Equipment

Each employee has a one-time home-office budget of 600 US dollars for a chair, desk, or monitor. Laptops and phones are issued by IT and must not be bought through expenses.
