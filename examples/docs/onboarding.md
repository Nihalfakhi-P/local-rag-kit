# Northwind Robotics — New Joiner Onboarding

*Fictional sample document for local-rag-kit demos.*

## First week

On day one, new joiners collect their laptop and security key from the IT desk on the 2nd floor between 09:00 and 11:00. Your manager schedules a welcome lunch during the first week.

Every new joiner is assigned an onboarding buddy from a different team. The buddy meets with you twice a week for the first month.

## Probation

The probation period is 3 months. Your manager holds a check-in at 30 days and 60 days, and a probation review at 90 days. The notice period during probation is 2 weeks; after probation it is 1 month.

## Mandatory training

All new joiners must complete security awareness training and the code of conduct course within their first 14 days. Engineers also complete the robot safety certification within their first 30 days before working in the lab.

## Benefits enrolment

Health insurance enrolment must be completed within 30 days of the start date in the HR portal. The company matches pension contributions up to 5 percent of base salary.
